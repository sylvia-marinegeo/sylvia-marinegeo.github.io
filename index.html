GeoB1515-1: Carbonate Mineralogy of Equatorial Atlantic Sediments

An investigation of carbonate mineral assemblages in a 6.58-meter marine sediment core from the western equatorial Atlantic Ocean.

Using X-ray diffraction data, Python-based analysis, and geospatial visualization, this project examines changes in sediment mineralogy and their potential paleoceanographic significance.

Oceanographic Investigation · Ongoing Research

Argo Float 1900857: An Anticyclone Encounter

An exploratory investigation of Southern Ocean hydrographic variability that began with an unexpected temperature and salinity anomaly in an Argo float profile.

Analysis of repeated vertical profiles, satellite altimetry, and the AVISO META4.0 eddy atlas revealed a prolonged association between the float and a tracked anticyclone in the Agulhas retroflection region during late 2009 and early 2010.

The investigation is presented in two parts: the discovery and identification of the anticyclone, followed by an examination of hydrographic evolution during the encounter.
