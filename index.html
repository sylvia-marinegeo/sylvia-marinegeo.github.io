Featured Research

GeoB1515-1 | Marine Sediment Core Analysis

Investigation of carbonate mineralogy and sediment composition in the western equatorial Atlantic. The project combines X-ray diffraction methodology, Python-based analysis, and QGIS mapping to explore sedimentary variability and its potential paleoceanographic significance.

The project is being prepared for public presentation while the underlying XRD dataset remains unpublished.
